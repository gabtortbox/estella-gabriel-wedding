Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the RSVP deadline?
Please RSVP by 31 January 2027, about two months before the wedding. This helps us finalise numbers with the venue.
Can I update my RSVP later?
Yes, anytime. Just open your personal RSVP link again, your previous answers will be loaded automatically. Make any changes you need and click Save to update your response. We'll always have the latest version.
Can I bring a +1?
We've kept the guest list small and intentional, so the names on your invite are the seats we have for you. If you have a special circumstance, please reach out to us directly and we'll do our best.
Are children welcome?
While we absolutely love your little ones, our celebration will include alcohol, loud music, live performances, and a lively crowd that may be overwhelming for young children. The venue itself also has limited capacity for us to comfortably accommodate everyone.

We kindly ask that children stay comfy at home so everyone can be fully present in the moment. If you have a special circumstance and absolutely need to bring your little one along, please let us know in advance and we'll do our best to accommodate. Thank you so much for understanding.
When should I arrive?
You're welcome to arrive anytime from 6 pm onwards for drinks and mingling. Please be seated by 6:45 pm as the walk-in begins shortly after.
What should I wear?
The vibe is formal but funky, loud or pastel colours, depending on our florals (we'll confirm closer to the day). The venue can get cold, so we'd recommend bringing a jacket along. See the full details on the dress code section.
What's on the menu?
We'll be having a 6-course Chinese dinner. If you have any dietary requirements, please note them in your RSVP.
Will there be drinks?
Yes! We're planning to serve red & white wines, gin, vodka and a few other options. You're also welcome to bring your own, corkage is waived (to be confirmed). If you have a favourite bottle you'd like to share with us, bring it along.
How do I get to the venue?
We've got maps and step-by-step instructions for car, taxi/PHV and MRT in the directions section above. There's also a QR code for free Sentosa entry.
Can we park at the venue?
Valet at Oasia Resort Sentosa is complimentary for all guests. Drive straight to Oasia and the valet team will take care of the rest.

If you'd rather self-park, the closest car park is at Village Hotel Sentosa, a short walk to Oasia. We've been allocated parking coupons equivalent to 20% of our guaranteed attendance, available on a first come, first served basis for self-park guests.
Where can I find nearby accommodation?
The most convenient hotels are right next door, Oasia Resort Sentosa (our venue) and Village Hotel Sentosa. Other nearby options on Sentosa include Shangri-La Rasa Sentosa and Capella Singapore, if you fancy making a weekend of it.
Still have a question? Drop us a message anytime.
